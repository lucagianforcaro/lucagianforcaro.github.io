About

I am a biomedical engineer with a background in biomechanics and wearable robotics, now focusing on robots that learn their behaviour from data.

For my MSc thesis at the BioRobotics Institute of Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna, I worked on arm and hand synergic control for functional electrical stimulation on a hybrid exoskeleton. I built the full pipeline, from synchronised data acquisition between an upper limb exoskeleton and a sensorised glove to deep learning models that decode hand motion intent from arm kinematics, and I prepared the models for deployment on embedded hardware with ROS 2.

Today I am studying robot learning in depth, from classical control to imitation and reinforcement learning, working hands on with PyTorch, MuJoCo and LeRobot. I am particularly interested in perception and in vision language action models. I am looking for junior roles in robotics and machine learning engineering in Europe.
